Your data, your control

Request data deletion

Motezy does not require an account. TV details, room names, pairing credentials, preferences and optional feature counters stay on your device and can be removed by deleting each TV in Motezy or uninstalling the app.

Delete purchase data

  1. Email pepper1848@hotmail.com with the subject “Motezy data deletion request”.
  2. Include the anonymous RevenueCat App User ID shown in Motezy’s support diagnostics. Do not send passwords, pairing tokens or payment-card details.
  3. We will delete the associated anonymous Motezy entitlement/customer record within 30 days and confirm completion by email.

What may be retained

Apple or Google may retain App Store or Google Play transaction records required for billing, refunds, fraud prevention, tax or legal obligations. Those records are controlled by the store and can be managed through your Apple or Google account. Motezy does not receive your payment-card details.

Questions

For any privacy question, email pepper1848@hotmail.com.
